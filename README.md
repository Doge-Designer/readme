## 🚀 About

Welcome to the only repo that contains more Dogecoin memes, Starlink victory laps, and "Elon is based" screenshots than your browser history.

I am DogeDesigner. I design things. Sometimes those things go viral. Sometimes Elon replies. Sometimes governments install Starlink because of a post. It's a living.

This is the media dump — the images, clips, and screenshots that live on [x.com/cb_doge/media](https://x.com/cb_doge/media) but now also live here so future archaeologists can study the peak of 2020s internet.

## ✨ Features

- 📸 Screenshots of posts that made it to 100k+ likes (and the ones that didn't, we don't talk about those)
- 🎬 Short videos of Starlink saving people in floods, mountains, and places with no signal
- 🎨 Design assets, mockups, and "I made this in 20 minutes" graphics
- 📈 Random X metrics that prove the platform is doing numbers
- 🐕 Occasional doge with sunglasses holding a color palette (it's me)
